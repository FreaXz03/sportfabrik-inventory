01 · Goods flow

The path of a delivery

Every item takes the same road. Stock is only added once goods have physically arrived. Tap a step.

Two places where it goes wrong easily. An order confirmation only creates an expected receipt — never stock. And goods at GEWA, VEBO or Dietikon get no receipt date until they reach a branch.

06 · Principles

What holds everywhere

Ten hard rules. These are the ones staff and management feel most.

What we consciously do without

AI on documents

Own parser, own server. An unknown layout is reported, never guessed.

Off-the-shelf software

It would not map our 35 categories, the 30/50/70 logic and the external stores — and it costs every month.

Assumed stock

Booked only when goods arrive. An order confirmation is an announcement, nothing more.
