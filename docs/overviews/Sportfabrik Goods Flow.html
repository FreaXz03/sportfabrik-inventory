Applies everywhere

  • Documents Stay LocalDocuments only reads our own parser, no AI, no cloud. No external CDNs — runs on the store network even without Internet.
  • Never override stockEvery change is a line in the stock movements, the stock is derived from it.
  • EAN is optionalVariants work without an EAN; the key is supplier + item number + color + size.
  • Shared Item MasterOne item master for all branches — only stock, receipts and reductions are branch-related.
  • Categories as in the cash registerMain category × sporting activity: 35 combinations; Cycling and Food have no sporting activity.
  • RightsEmployees may manually receive stock, correct stock levels, and book out sales (only sales, since 28.09.2026), all only in their assigned branches. Other stock removals, cancellations, and stock transfers are reserved for branch managers and headquarters. Markdowns are changed only in one's own branches; headquarters may change all. Their existing rights to record transactions across branches remain in place; read permissions are unchanged.
