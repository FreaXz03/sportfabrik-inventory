Sportfabrik Inventory Management

Each item is recorded once upon receipt — by uploading the receipt (invoice, delivery note, order confirmation) or by hand with the scanner. After that, the system permanently knows which item was delivered at which price and when and how much of it is in which store. Self-built, runs on our own server, without license fees — receipts never leave the house.

What we consciously do without

  • No AI for DocumentsEach receipt is read by our own parser on our own server. No bill, no delivery note leaves the house. An unknown layout is reported, not guessed.
  • No standard softwareIt would never accurately map our checkout categories, the 30/50/70 logic and the external locations GEWA, VEBO and Dietikon – and costs continuously.
  • No Assumed StockIt is only booked when the goods have actually arrived. An order confirmation is just an announcement.
